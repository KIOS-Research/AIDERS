About AIDERS

Real-time Artificial Intelligence for DEcision support via RPAS data analyticS

Our Mission

AIDERS is a cutting-edge platform designed to revolutionize disaster management and emergency response. We combine artificial intelligence, drone technology, and real-time data analysis to provide comprehensive solutions for crisis management, search and rescue operations, and environmental monitoring.

Our platform empowers first responders, emergency management teams, and organizations with the tools they need to make informed decisions quickly and effectively during critical situations.

UAV Management

Advanced drone control and coordination system for aerial surveillance, mapping, and emergency response operations.

Computer Vision

AI-powered object detection and recognition capabilities for real-time analysis of visual data from multiple sources.

Live Mapping

Real-time mapping and geographic information system integration for comprehensive situational awareness.

Data Analytics

Advanced analytics and reporting tools for mission planning, performance analysis, and decision support.

Sensor Integration

Multi-sensor data fusion including environmental sensors, LiDAR, and IoT device integration.

Team Collaboration

Multi-user operation management with role-based access control and real-time collaboration tools.

Developed By

AIDERS is developed by the KIOS Research and Innovation Center of Excellence at the University of Cyprus. Our team consists of leading researchers and engineers in the fields of artificial intelligence, robotics, emergency management, and systems engineering.

This project is funded by the European Union Civil Protection mechanism, supporting innovative solutions for disaster preparedness and response.
